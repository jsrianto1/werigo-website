# WERIGO Journey Loop identity

Selected identity: Concept 04, final brand system dated 16 November 2026.

Use the exact vector masters in `public/brand/journey-loop-v20261119/`: teal or white wordmark, and the matching open-loop symbol. Do not retype, trace, stretch, filter or close the final o. Wordmark minimum width: 120 px; symbol minimum: 24 px. Keep clear space around the artwork. App icons use 20% margins.

Core palette: Deep Petrol `#061E20`, Journey Teal `#00594F`, Warm Ivory `#FAF6EE`, White `#FFFFFF`, Sea Mist `#89AFA7`. Sea Mist is a supporting colour, not small text on white. Semantic warning and error colours remain distinct.

Typography: local Geist 400, 500 and 700, licensed under the included OFL. Existing fonts provide fallbacks, including Cyrillic.

Shared identity: `BrandLockup.tsx`, global `journey-loop.css`, and scoped PWA/Ops `journey-loop.css`. Use the native white artwork on dark surfaces and the teal artwork on light surfaces. WEDISON endorsement files are original, unchanged artwork. No extra tagline belongs beneath the logo.

Legacy logo/icon URLs remain available with refreshed artwork. New references use versioned filenames to avoid stale cached images. The PWA start URL remains `/app/login`; offline caching remains limited to the generic reconnect page.

Product photography, SAGA panels and video, rental pricing, stock, bookings, payments, authentication and environment settings are outside this identity update.
